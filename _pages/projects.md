### 🌫 SmogCast — Air Quality Classification & Forecasting

[GitHub Repository](https://github.com/silverfrost702/SmogCast-Predicting-and-Forecasting-Air-Quality-in-India){: .btn .btn--primary }
[Live Streamlit Demo](https://smogcast.streamlit.app){: .btn .btn--success }

Developed an **end-to-end data science system** for real-time air quality classification and **7-day forecasting of PM2.5 / PM10 levels** across major Indian cities.

Engineered **temporal and lag-based features** and trained **machine learning and deep learning models** to capture pollution dynamics. Deployed an **interactive Streamlit dashboard** for real-time monitoring and data-driven decision support.

#### 📈 Model Performance
- **Multiclass AQI Classification Accuracy:** **93%**
- **Time-Series Forecasting RMSE:** **9–12**

**Skills & Tools:**  
Machine Learning · Time Series Forecasting · Deep Learning · Feature Engineering · Streamlit · Data Visualization · Python
