### 🌫 SmogCast — Air Quality Classification & Forecasting  
🔗 https://github.com/silverfrost702/SmogCast-Predicting-and-Forecasting-Air-Quality-in-India
🚀 Live Demo: (https://smogcast.streamlit.app/)

Developed an **end-to-end data science system** for real-time air quality classification and **7-day forecasting of PM2.5 / PM10 levels** across major Indian cities. Engineered temporal and lag-based features and trained machine learning and deep learning models to capture pollution dynamics. Deployed an **interactive Streamlit dashboard** for real-time monitoring and decision support.

**Model Performance:**  
- Multiclass AQI Classification Accuracy: **93%**  
- Time-Series Forecasting RMSE: **9–12**

**Skills & Tools:**  
Machine Learning · Time Series Forecasting · Deep Learning · Feature Engineering · Streamlit · Data Visualization · Python
